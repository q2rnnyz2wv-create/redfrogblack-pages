01, The studio

A small studio, three growing brands.

RedFrogBlack Limited is an independent London company. We make original paintings, cognitive-science learning tools, and family-friendly games. Each brand stands on its own, and shares a house style: colourful, precise, quiet in the details.

01

Original, not derivative

Every piece, painting, question, world and puzzle is made from scratch. No stock, no shortcuts.

02

Considered, not rushed

New work ships when it is ready. If a thing is worth making, it is worth making properly.

03

Colourful, not shouty

We love colour. We use it deliberately, never for the sake of noise.

04

Kind, by default

No dark patterns. No forced ads. No pay-to-win. Products we would want our own family to use.
