What the app does

Rise or Else rings an alarm and asks you to finish a short task to switch it off. It can be linked to a parent's or buddy's phone, which can see wake-up activity and switch the alarm off remotely.

Free and Plus

The free version works on one phone with two missions. Rise or Else Plus adds linked phones, all missions, app locking, alerts and history, for the whole household.

Please use it sensibly

  • Phones can fail: a flat battery, a switched-off or broken phone, system updates or settings can stop any alarm. Don't rely on Rise or Else alone when missing a wake-up would be serious, such as a flight, exam or medicine.
  • Missions involve moving around: take care getting out of bed, especially in the dark.
  • Parents: link only a phone you are responsible for, and agree it with your teen. Don't use the app to monitor anyone without their knowledge.
