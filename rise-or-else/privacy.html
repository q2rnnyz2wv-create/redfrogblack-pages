Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

This policy explains what Rise or Else ("the app") collects, why, and your rights. The app is made by RedFrogBlack Limited ("we", "us"), the data controller under UK GDPR. Contact: info@redfrogblackart.com.

The short version

  • No sign-up, no email address or phone number needed.
  • No adverts, no tracking across other apps or websites, no selling of data.
  • We keep only what the alarm and the linked phone need to work.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

DataWhy
A first name or nickname you type inSo linked phones can show who is who
Alarm settings: time, days, missions, rulesTo ring the alarm and apply the parent's rules
Wake-up records: when the alarm rang, when the mission started and finished, whether the phone went offline, help requestsTo show the linked parent what happened and the history chart
Kitchen and emergency codesTo check codes during the morning mission
A random device identifier and a secret key created by the appTo recognise your phone without an account
A push notification token (parent phones only)To send alerts such as "still no response"
The language your phone usesSo alerts and the activity feed arrive in your language
Purchase status (from Apple or Google, via RevenueCat)To unlock paid features

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or which apps you use. App locking uses Apple's Screen Time: the choice of apps stays on your phone and is never sent to us.

Legal basis

We use this data to provide the service you asked for (contract), and to keep it secure (legitimate interests).

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase (database hosting, London, UK) stores the data above.
  • Expo delivers push notifications to parent phones.
  • RevenueCat, Apple and Google handle subscriptions and payments. We never see card details.

Each acts under contract and only on our instructions. Where data leaves the UK, it is protected by approved safeguards.

Children and teenagers

The teen version is meant to be set up by a parent. A teen's phone shares only its nickname and wake-up records with the linked parent phone. If you are under 13, ask a parent to set up the app. We do not knowingly collect more than described here from anyone, and we never use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

For as long as you use the app. Deleting your data in the app removes it from our database straight away. Backups roll over within 7 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email info@redfrogblackart.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
