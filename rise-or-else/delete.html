Delete your data

You can delete your Rise or Else data at any time. There is no account to close: each phone has its own data.

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open Rise or Else.
  2. Parent phone: go to Rules and tap Delete my data. This deletes the whole household: settings, codes, wake-up history and links to other phones.
  3. Other phone: go to Settings and tap Delete my data. This removes that phone and its history only.

Data is removed from our database straight away. Backups roll over within 7 days.

Without the app

Email info@redfrogblackart.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the nickname used in the app. We'll confirm within 7 days.

Subscriptions

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel in your Apple or Google account settings.
