Help and support

Rise or Else is a wake-up alarm that won't switch off until you've done a short morning mission. A parent (or a grown-up "wake-up buddy") can link their phone to see when the alarm rings, whether you're up, and step in if needed.

Need help? Email info@redfrogblackart.com. We reply within two working days.

Common questions

How do I link two phones? On the parent phone choose "I'm the parent", then show the link code. On the other phone choose "This phone needs waking up" and type the code. Codes work once, for 24 hours.

The alarm didn't ring. Open Rise or Else once after installing and allow alarms and notifications. On iPhone, keep the app installed and don't turn off "Alarms" in Settings › Rise or Else. On Android, allow "Alarms & reminders" and turn off battery optimisation for the app.

I'm stuck on a mission. Tap "Can't do it?" to ask the linked parent to switch it off, or enter one of the six emergency codes from the parent's Codes screen. Each emergency code works once.

Why are my apps locked? If the parent turned on app locking, chosen apps stay locked from alarm time until the mission is done. This uses Apple's Screen Time and was approved on the phone by the parent.

How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google. iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Android: Play Store › profile › Payments and subscriptions › Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds. Apple and Google handle refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com or Google Play refunds.

Restore a purchase on a new phone. Open the upgrade screen and tap "Restore purchases".

Delete my data. In the app: Settings › Delete my data. Or see how to delete your data.
