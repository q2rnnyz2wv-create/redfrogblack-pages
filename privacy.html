1. Who we are

This policy is issued by REDFROGBLACK LIMITED (“RedFrogBlack”, “we”, “us”), a private company registered in England and Wales under company number 16557982. We trade under names including RedFrogBlack and OmniModi. We are the data controller for the personal data described here, unless we say otherwise below.

Contact for all privacy matters: info@redfrogblackart.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers our websites, web apps and the apps we publish on the Apple App Store and Google Play, including:

  • OmniModi – a GCSE revision app, including its AI Teacher feature;
  • RFB Books – Ribbz (reading and speech practice for children, including the Logopedie speech-practice module) and RFB Library (ebooks and audiobooks for teenagers and adults);
  • RedFrogBlack Studio – our book-production tool for the RedFrogBlack team and authors;
  • CroakDown – our game;
  • AI Meeting Place – our AI conversation app;
  • our merchandise shop and our websites (including redfrogblack.com and redfrogblackart.com).

Some products also have their own, more detailed privacy notice shown in the app (for example the Ribbz, RFB Library and Studio policies at studio.redfrogblack.com/privacy and OmniModi's policy in the OmniModi app). Where a product notice gives more detail, it adds to this policy; if the two ever conflict, the more protective statement applies.

3. What we collect, product by product

All products

  • Technical data: like every internet service, requests to our servers carry your device's IP address and basic technical details (such as browser or app version). Our hosting providers use these to deliver the service and keep it secure, in short-lived server logs. We do not use them to identify you or for advertising.
  • Error reports: if something breaks, an app may send a short technical message about the error. We remove anything personal before it is kept.
  • Messages to us: if you e-mail us or use a help or feedback form, we keep your message, your contact details and our reply to deal with your request.

OmniModi (GCSE revision and AI Teacher)

  • Without an account: your study data stays in your browser's storage on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Age band: at first use you choose under 13, 13–17 or 18 and over. If you are under 13, no account option is offered.
  • With an account (13+): your e-mail address and sign-in details (handled by our sign-in provider, Clerk; we do not see your password), and your study set-up (subjects, exam boards, plan, age band, preferences), Planner tasks, study progress, exam-practice results, games history, and highlights or notes you add – so they follow you between devices. Free-text Planner task titles you type are stripped out before syncing.
  • AI Teacher: where the AI Teacher is available to you, the messages you send it are processed by an AI provider to generate a reply. Before your first message can reach an AI provider, the app shows a notice naming that provider. We do not allow AI providers to use your messages to train their models where their terms give us that choice.
  • Why: to provide revision tools and keep your progress in sync.

RFB Books: Ribbz (including Logopedie) and RFB Library

  • On the device only: app language and settings, children's profiles in Ribbz (a first name or nickname and, optionally, an age), reading and speech-practice progress, the last page read, downloaded books and what the device has unlocked. In RFB Library, the answer to the 18+ check (yes/no only – never the date of birth).
  • Install number: only when a grown-up enters a code, buys something, or opens the plans in a store version, the app creates a random install number and secret. We keep the install number, a scrambled copy of the secret, the app, when it was created, the last day used and what was unlocked. It contains no name, e-mail, age, device identifier or location.
  • Optional account: e-mail address and sign-in method (Sign in with Apple, Google, e-mail and password, or a sign-in link); your name only if you type it; in Ribbz each child's first name or nickname and age (never a date of birth); device names (such as “iPad”) and when last used; places in books, bookmarks, settings and reading statistics (for Ribbz speech practice: counts of words read, skipped or helped, and up to 20 words to practise – never audio or transcripts). The statistics are only shown to you.
  • Microphone (Ribbz “Read with me” and speech practice): used only after a parent agrees and only while the microphone button is lit. We record and store nothing. Speech recognition is done by the device or browser; in the App Store and Google Play versions only on-device recognition is used. In a web browser that cannot recognise speech on the device, the browser may send the sound to its own speech service (for example Google, Apple or Microsoft) under its own privacy policy. RFB Library does not use the microphone.
  • Purchases: see “Payments” below.
  • Why: to deliver the books and features you unlocked and, if you choose, sync your family's reading across devices.

RedFrogBlack Studio (adults only, 18+)

  • Account and team: e-mail address, a scrambled form of your password (or that you used Google or Apple sign-in), sign-up and sign-in times and sign-in security logs (IP address and browser), your name or nickname if given, your role in each workspace and invitations.
  • Your content: books, pages, notes, comments, pictures, recordings, exports and change history. For personal data inside content an author uploads, the author decides what goes in and we act as the author's processor.
  • Voice recordings and the narrator consent record kept with them. A child's voice may be recorded only with written consent from a parent or guardian.
  • Usage and plans: monthly AI voice and translation usage; plan and Stripe customer/subscription references when payments are switched on.
  • Support access: a time-limited, logged, read-only support view only when you ask for help.
  • AI features: text is sent to Microsoft Azure AI Translator or Azure AI Speech only when you start a translation or AI voice, and only the part being processed. Optional team-only tools may use ElevenLabs (AI voice) or Anthropic (to summarise reviewer comments on our own books); these are not used for authors' workspaces. Your books are not used to train AI.

CroakDown

We collect only what is needed to run the game, such as game settings and progress, technical data and, if you buy something, the purchase record described under “Payments”. If CroakDown offers an account or online features, the data used is limited to running those features, and the in-game or store privacy information will tell you what is collected.

AI Meeting Place

AI Meeting Place lets you hold conversations with AI. To provide it, the text (and any other content) you send is processed by our servers and by the AI model provider(s) that generate the replies, and your account details and conversation history are stored so you can return to them. The app tells you which AI providers are used. Do not share sensitive personal information in conversations. We do not use your conversations for advertising and do not sell them.

Merchandise shop

When you order from our shop we collect your name, delivery address, e-mail address, phone number if given, and what you ordered, to take payment, make and deliver your order, handle returns and keep accounting records. Payment card details are entered on the payment provider's checkout and are not seen or stored by us. Your order details are shared with the providers that run the shop, produce and deliver the goods, only for that purpose.

Payments (all products)

  • App Store and Google Play: Apple or Google takes the payment under their own terms and privacy policies; we never see card details. For RFB Books, purchases are confirmed to us by RevenueCat using only the random install number and the store's transaction details.
  • On the web: you pay on Stripe's checkout page. Stripe handles the payment details you enter; we receive only references to what was bought, never card details.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We do not show behavioural advertising or use advertising networks in our apps.
  • We do not profile children or nudge them to spend more time or money.
  • We do not collect precise location.

4. Why we use data (legal bases under UK GDPR)

  • Contract: to provide the app, account, content, purchases and orders you ask for.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep our services secure and working (server logs, error reports, abuse prevention, support you request), using as little data as possible.
  • Consent: for the microphone in Ribbz (given by a parent), voice recordings in the Studio, and any optional feature that asks for it. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: to keep payment, tax and accounting records.

6. How long we keep data

  • Data on your device: until you delete it, uninstall the app or clear browser storage.
  • Accounts: while your account is open; then deleted as described in section 7.
  • RFB Books install numbers: while in use; ended at once and removed within about 30 days after “Forget this device”; removed after 180 days without use if never used for a code or purchase. Reading statistics older than 12 months become monthly totals.
  • OmniModi: after you delete your account, your learning data is kept for 7 days (so a genuine mistake can be put right) and then permanently erased.
  • Studio: workspaces until the owner deletes them (files removed at once, records within about 30 days); usage events 25 months; older versions 180 days (except the newest 50 of each edition).
  • Payment, invoice and order records: six years, as UK tax law requires.
  • Error report counts and code-entry attempts: 90 days.
  • Hosting and sign-in logs: as long as the provider keeps them, typically days to weeks.
  • Backups: our hosting providers keep short-lived disaster-recovery backups that expire on their schedule.
  • Support e-mails: as long as needed to deal with your request and any follow-up.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and personal data at any time, without needing to contact us:

  • In the app: use “Delete my account” (in OmniModi under Settings; in Ribbz and RFB Library under My account, then My data; in Ribbz a grown-up passes the grown-ups check first).
  • Without the app (web or e-mail route): e-mail info@redfrogblackart.com with the subject “Delete my data”, from the e-mail address of your account, naming the app. For apps used without an account, include the install number shown under My account or the store's order number. Step-by-step instructions for RFB Books and the Studio are at studio.redfrogblack.com/delete-data. We confirm within 30 days.

What happens:

  • RFB Books (Ribbz, RFB Library): children's names, settings, bookmarks and heard words are overwritten immediately; the rest of your family's data is removed from our servers within 1 day; your login is removed 3 days after your request. Every device signs out.
  • OmniModi: your account closes immediately; your learning data is permanently erased after 7 days.
  • Studio: owners can delete a workspace in the Studio; sign-in accounts are closed within 30 days of an e-mail request.
  • Other products: we delete your account data within 30 days of your request.
  • What we keep: payment and order records we must keep for tax law (six years). Records held by Apple, Google or Stripe are kept under their terms.
  • Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel an app-store subscription. Cancel it in your Apple Account settings or Google Play (Payments and subscriptions).

8. Children's privacy

Some of our products are designed for children (Ribbz) or used by teenagers (OmniModi, RFB Library's teen shelf). We design them to follow the UK Information Commissioner's Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children's Code) and UK GDPR: high privacy by default, as little data as possible, data kept on the device where we can, no profiling, no advertising, and a grown-ups check in front of settings, purchases and anything that leads outside a children's app.

  • Parental consent: in the UK a child under 13 cannot consent to online data processing alone (the age is set by national law elsewhere in Europe, for example 15 in the Czech Republic). Where consent is needed for a child, we ask a parent or guardian – for example for the microphone in Ribbz. Parents set up Ribbz and any optional family account.
  • Under 13s in OmniModi are not offered an account; their data stays on their device.
  • RedFrogBlack Studio and the RFB Library adult shelf are for adults (18+).
  • United States (COPPA): we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 as defined by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act without verifiable parental consent. Ribbz does not ask for or receive a child's contact details, photos, voice recordings or location; the only identifier is a random install number used to deliver what was unlocked or bought.

If you believe we hold information about your child that we should not, write to info@redfrogblackart.com and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Under UK GDPR (and, where it applies, EU GDPR) you have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data (portability), to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time. Many apps let you do this yourself (for example “Download my data” and “Delete my account”). Otherwise e-mail info@redfrogblackart.com; we reply within one month. We may need to confirm your identity first.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or the data protection authority where you live (in the Czech Republic: Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz). We would like the chance to help first.

Residents of other places, including US states with privacy laws, may have similar rights; contact us and we will honour them as the law requires. We do not sell or “share” personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising.

10. International transfers

Where possible we store data in the UK (for example our RFB Books database with Supabase in London). Some providers, such as Vercel, Stripe, RevenueCat, Clerk, AI providers and e-mail delivery, are based in or process data in the United States or elsewhere. Where personal data leaves the UK or the European Economic Area, we rely on approved safeguards in the providers' data-processing terms, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or the UK–US data bridge where the provider is certified.

11. Security

We protect data with encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls that limit who can see data, scrambled (hashed) secrets and passwords, logged and time-limited support access, and reputable hosting providers. No service is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data and puts you at risk, we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

12. Cookies and device storage

We use only cookies and device storage (such as browser local storage) that are strictly necessary to run our services: keeping you signed in, remembering your language, theme and settings, saving progress and downloaded content on your device, and remembering purchases. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies. Because these are strictly necessary, they do not require consent; if we ever add non-essential cookies, we will ask first. Our web pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and the “Last updated” date when we make changes. If a change matters (for example a new kind of data), we will tell account holders by e-mail or in the app before it takes effect, and in children's apps we will ask a parent again before anything new is used.
